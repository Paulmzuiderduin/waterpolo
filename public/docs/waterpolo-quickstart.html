Shotmap Quickstart

From team setup to your first shot.

  1. Sign in or create an account.
  2. Create or select a season and team through Workspace → Seasons & teams.
  3. Open Match setup, then Roster to add players.
  4. Create a new match, then open Lineup to select its players.
  5. Click the field to record a shot, or use + Penalty. Select the player, result, situation, period, and time, then save.
  6. Use Fullscreen for more field space. Edit or delete shots from the shot list.
  7. Select Whole season to review multiple matches, and use Filters or Show analysis as needed.
  8. Use Export to download a field image or CSV.
