Water Polo Shot Logging

Record water polo shots during a match.

Waterpolo Hub focuses on shot logging: location, player, goal or save or miss, attacking situation, period, and time.

During the match

Select your match and lineup, then click the field to enter each shot. Fullscreen provides more space on phones, tablets, and computers. Record opponent goals through Correct score.

What is covered

This is a shotmap tool. It does not provide a general foul, turnover, or possession event logger. Goals come from recorded shots, with score corrections available when needed.
